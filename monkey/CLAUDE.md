# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Who you are talking to

This game belongs to Jaś, a young kid who is making his own browser game. He decides what the game becomes; you help him build it.

- **Always reply in Polish**, even if the question comes in another language.
- Use short sentences and simple words a young kid understands. No jargon: say "pętla gry działa w kółko", not "requestAnimationFrame loop".
- Explain what you changed in one or two sentences, then tell him how to see it (refresh the page).
- Be encouraging. Suggest one small next idea at the end, never a long list.
- After every finished change, commit it with git (only this game's files, short Polish message describing what Jaś asked for), so nothing is lost. Jaś calls this "zapisywanie".
- Make small changes, one idea at a time, so he can see each effect. If his idea is big, split it into steps and do the first one.
- Ask a simple question when an idea is unclear ("Czy wróg ma gonić małpkę, czy chodzić w kółko?").

## The game

An original top-down open-world adventure (Jaś is inspired by the Zelda games he plays on Nintendo). Keep it original: no names, characters, art, music or maps from Zelda or other existing games, even when he asks for "like in Zelda". Offer an own version instead (own hero, own items, own places).

## How to run

Tooling lives in `mise.toml`. Every reusable command (run, reload, check, anything repeated) goes there as a mise task, not into ad-hoc scripts or docs.

- `mise run serwer` - local server with live reload (`live-server` on port 8080): the browser refreshes itself after each file save. Use this while developing.
- `mise run graj` - just opens `index.html` in the browser, no server.

The game itself needs no build: plain HTML/JS that also works by double-clicking `index.html`. Do not add `package.json`, bundlers, frameworks or ES modules (`type="module"` breaks when opened as a file). Node tools are installed through mise `[tools]` only. New code goes in plain `<script>` tags in `index.html`, loaded in order.

## Code structure

3D with Three.js (WebGL). The library is vendored at `biblioteki/three.min.js` (r128, the classic build that exposes a global `THREE`; newer versions ship only as ES modules). Use APIs available in r128 and don't upgrade without switching the whole project to modules.

- `mapa.js` - the worlds, meant for Jaś to edit by hand. `MAPY` holds one map per level (7 levels); each map is an array of equal-length strings, one letter per tile; `KAFELKI` maps each letter to ground `kolor`, optional `rzecz` (what stands on the tile) and `sciana` (blocks movement). Every map needs one `S` (start), one `M` (fighting staff) guarded by three `Z` warriors, and bananas `B`, all reachable from `S`. A 2x2 square of `D` becomes one big house (`postawDomek`), a lone `D` a small one. New terrain or object = new letter in `KAFELKI` plus, for a new `rzecz`, a builder in `BUDOWNICZY` in `game.js`.
- `game.js` - tile `(k, w)` sits at world `x = k*KAFELEK, z = w*KAFELEK`, y is up. Models are `THREE.Group`s of simple shapes made with `klocek(geometry, kolor, x, y, z)` (materials are `MeshStandardMaterial`s cached per colour in `farba`, so clone a material before changing it on one object; colours go through `kolorek()` because the renderer uses sRGB output and ACES tone mapping). Surfaces get canvas-drawn textures (`zrobFakture`: ground, fur, skin, warrior skin, fabric); `podmienFarby(model, [[kolor, material]])` swaps a model's plain colours for textured or glossy ones. The sky is a vertex-coloured dome (`niebo`) that follows the camera; metal (`farbaMetalu`) reflects the canvas cube map `odbicieSwiata`, no model or texture files. `zbudujPoziom()` rebuilds the world of level `poziom` into the `kawalkiSwiata` group from `powiekszMape(MAPY[poziom - 1])`, so tile indices include the added ring; The enemies are wolf warriors (`zrobWojownika`, map letter `Z`): upright wolves with a random coat (`UMASZCZENIA_WILKOW`), digitigrade legs, clawed paws (`zrobLape`), tribal paint, fang necklace, swinging tail and an opening jaw; about 15% are bigger alpha leaders with a bone crown. `animujWilka` wags the tail, snaps the jaw while running and, when a warrior first spots the hero, makes it stop and howl at the sky (`wojownik.wyje`). `POZIOMY` sets warrior speed and sight per level. A level is won by collecting all bananas, or by defeating every warrior while holding at least `BANANY_Z_WOJOWNIKAMI` (5) bananas; the HUD shows bananas and living warriors. Each level has `5 + poziom` warriors: after building the map, `dodajWojownikow` adds the missing ones on reachable grass or sand at least 14 m from the start. `dodajWojownikow(8, true)` adds 8 more on the new terrain only. The hero has `WSZYSTKIE_ZYCIA` (5) hearts, which may be fractional (HUD shows ❤️ / 💔 half / 🖤). A warrior's claw costs one heart via `zranKiko(1, ...)`: the hero is knocked back, stays where she is and blinks for `CZAS_NIETYKALNOSCI` steps of invulnerability (`malpka.nietykalna`, `mrugajPoZranieniu`); losing the last heart restarts at level 1. A hit warrior staggers (`trafionyWojownik`, `chwianie`); with one life left it staggers all the time and walks slower. Big houses (2x2 `D`) have a door on the south side that opens when the hero is near; walking into it takes the hero into the house interior (`domy.js`), where warriors and predators can't reach her. Keep the two tiles south of a big house walkable. The hero is a monkey named Kiko (`IMIE_MALPKI`, shown in the HUD and used in messages) wearing a big straw hat (`model.kapelusz`) a white kimono with a black belt (long sleeves from `dodajRekaw` with Chinese-character patches `naszywka`, clean `fakturaPlotna`) and a black ninja mask over nose and mouth. The hero `malpka` keeps its state (`x`, `z`, `kat` facing, `wysokosc`, lives, staff) separate from its `model`, whose limbs are pivot groups (`reka`, `lewaReka`, `nogi`, `ogon`) rotated for animation; warriors have `nogi` and `rece` pivots the same way, and their glowing eyes use `MeshBasicMaterial` (no `emissive`, so the hit flash skips them). Mouse (pointer lock) and left/right keys turn the hero, vertical mouse movement sets `malpka.patrz` so the camera can look up at the sky (the sun disc is a child of `niebo` in the light's direction `KIERUNEK_SLONCA`), up/down walk along `kat`; the hero walks with the bamboo fighting staff (`zrobPatyk`) upright at her side; clicking (or holding) the left mouse button spins her once around with the staff held out (`uderz`, `DLUGOSC_ZAMACHU` steps, white ring `slad`), and mid-spin `trafPatykiem` hits every warrior within `ZASIEG_PATYKA` all around (2 lives per hit, so 2 hits per warrior) and only scares predators; animals are never hurt. Holding Shift (or being mid-jump, e.g. jumping off a bridge) lets the hero enter water, which calls `chlup` (splash drops and sound) (`moznaStanac(x, z, mozePlywac)`; warriors never swim); after 5 s of swimming `stracZycie()` takes a heart. Walking into a tree climbs it (`malpka.wspina`); at `WYSOKOSC_DRZEWA` the hero walks on treetops (`moznaStanac(..., naDrzewach)`, `wysokoscZiemi` returns the treetop height on `T`) and falls when stepping off. Outside the map `kafelekW` returns `"poza"`, which always blocks. `ustawKamere` pulls the camera closer when a tree, house or rock (`WYSOKOSC_PRZESZKOD`) is between it and the hero. `=` is a walkable bridge over water; a run of `=` tiles forms one arch (`lukMostu`), and `wysokoscZiemi(x, z)` gives the ground height that the hero and warriors stand on. Trees keep their crown in a `korona` pivot group; `animujSwiat()` sways crowns by `silaWiatru()` and `ruszLiscie()` drops leaves from trees near the hero. The game runs at a fixed 60 steps per second regardless of frame rate: `petlaGry()` runs as many `krokGry()` steps as the clock requires (at most 5 per frame), then renders; keep every per-step change (speeds, counters in frames) inside `krokGry`. Progress (level, hearts, staff) is saved to `localStorage` under `NAZWA_ZAPISU` every 5 s, on pause and when the page is hidden or closed; on load `wczytajGre()` resumes that level. F4 toggles `kameraZPrzodu` (the camera moves in front of the hero to show her face; controls stay the same). P toggles `pauza`, which stops the steps (and mouse turning, attacking, Q) and shows the `#pauza` overlay. Each step runs `animujSwiat()`, `ruszaj()`, `obslugaPatyka()`, `ruszWojownikow()`, `ruszZwierzeta()`, `ruszLudzi()`, then `ustawKamere()` (camera behind the hero's back, sun follows the hero).
- `teren.js` - `powiekszMape` adds a `RAMKA`-tile ring of new forest (or desert) around every map, opens the old border and stamps the village `PLAN_WIOSKI` (houses, sand roads, a well `U`) south of the old map; `wWiosce(w, k)` marks village tiles (no warriors or predators spawn there); `wybierzRosline` decorates grass and sand tiles with walk-through plants (bushes, ferns, tall grass, bamboo, palms, more palms next to water; only flowers in the village); `naNowymTerenie(w, k)` tells ring tiles apart. `zrobZiemie` draws all ground tiles as one `InstancedMesh` with per-tile colours (keep draw calls low: the world has thousands of objects), `zrobGestaTrawe` adds `KEPEK_NA_KAWALEK` grass tufts per meadow tile as one `InstancedMesh`, `zrobPlot` fences the walkable edge, `scalStatyczne` merges every static object pushed to `statyczne` (trees, rocks, houses except their `userData.ruchome` doors, decorations, bridges, burrows, the view) into one mesh per 24 m square and material, and tree crowns (`userData.kolysze`) sway in the vertex shader (`farbaNaWietrze`); anything that moves must stay out of `statyczne`. `zrobWidok` adds the unreachable view beyond it (big ground plane, trees, fog-free mountains).
- `zwierzeta.js` - forest animals (`GATUNKI`: rabbits that hide in burrows `nory`, deer that graze) and predators (`drapieznik: true`: tigers, a panther, gorillas that beat their chest first), placed by `dodajZwierzeta` (predators only on the new terrain, 25 m+ from the start) and moved by `ruszZwierzeta`, which also flies the circling birds (`ptaki`, `ruszPtaki`). `ruszDrapieznika` chases the hero unless she is on a treetop or in a house and takes half a heart on contact (`zranKiko(0.5, ...)`), then backs off; a bullet only scares a predator away (`przestraszZwierze`), it never kills animals.
- `skarby.js` - per level `dodajMapeISkarb` puts a rolled map next to the village well and the treasure chest on the `X` tile of the hideout `PLAN_KRYJOWKI` that `powiekszMape` stamps in the far top-right corner (rock ring with one gap, surrounded by a two-tile water moat, two wolf warriors inside). After picking up the map, M toggles the `#mapa` canvas (game paused via `mapaOtwarta`): `narysujTloMapy` draws terrain once per level, `narysujMape` adds the treasure cross, the throwing-star pedestal, stars lying on the ground, live wolf warriors and the hero. Opening the chest refills all hearts.
- `gwiazdki.js` - throwing stars: `powiekszMape` stamps `PLAN_POLANY` (stone pedestal `G` guarded by three wolf warriors) in the bottom-left corner; picking it up gives `GWIAZDKI_NA_START` (13) stars. E or the right mouse button throws one (`rzucGwiazdke`); a star hitting a warrior calls `trafWojownika` (3 hits kill), only scares predators, then drops to the ground (`lezaceGwiazdki`) where the hero can pick it up again (lost in water).
- `domy.js` - one shared house interior (`zrobPokoj`, built lazily at `POKOJ` far outside the world): wooden floor, three beds (`LOZKA`), table, rug, lanterns with a point light. `wejdzDoDomku` / `wyjdzZDomku` teleport the hero (and snap the camera, which hangs under the ceiling by the door while inside); walking back out through the door (`sprawdzWyjscie`) leaves. `moznaStanac` delegates to `wolneWPokoju` for coordinates near `POKOJ`. `pokazSpiacych` lays the villagers of the hero's current house in their beds with a floating "Zzz".
- `ludzie.js` - villagers (`zrobCzlowieka`: men, women (some in dresses) and children with random height, skin, eye colour, hairstyle, beard and straw hat; arms bend at `lokiec` and legs at `kolano`) placed by `dodajLudzi`; `ruszLudzi` runs a small state machine per villager (`stan`: `spacer` walks inside the village and waves at the hero, `doDomu` goes to a village house to nap, `ucieka` runs to the nearest house with a free bed when a predator is within 10 m, `wDomu` sleeps or hides and leaves only when no predator is near the door); `idzDo` walks around a house that blocks the way. Predators hunt villagers outside (`ofiara` in `zwierzeta.js`), sometimes wander toward the village, and back off after reaching one.
- `dzwieki.js` - sounds synthesized with Web Audio (`dzwiekChrup`, `dzwiekSwist`, `dzwiekTrafienie`, `dzwiekAu`, `dzwiekWycie`), no sound files.
- `index.html` / `style.css` - full-window `<canvas id="plansza">` with the HUD text laid over it, and the script tags (three, mapa, dzwieki, teren, zwierzeta, ludzie, domy, skarby, gwiazdki, then game; the files before game.js only declare things and use game.js helpers inside functions, because game.js builds the first level as soon as it loads).

## Code conventions

- Names of variables and functions in Polish without diacritics (`malpka`, `szybkosc`, `nowyBanan`), so Jaś can read them.
- Comments in Polish, short, explaining *what* a part does in kid language.
- Prefer simple, readable code over clever code. Avoid classes, arrow-function chains and abstractions he can't follow.
- If `game.js` grows large, split it into a few files (e.g. `postacie.js`, `rysowanie.js`) loaded with separate `<script>` tags in order.
- Images or sounds go in an `obrazki/` or `dzwieki/` folder.
